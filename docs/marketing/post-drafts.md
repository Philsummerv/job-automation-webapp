## The weekly-burden post

**Written from the owner's real situation, with everything identifying or
financial stripped out.** Deliberately absent, and it stays that way: benefit
amounts, the maximum payable, home address, prior salary, debts, savings, gig
work history, and anything touching the claim application or the disclosure
that followed it.

Note the framing: it says **"here's what my week looks like"**, not "here are
the rules." That's on purpose. He isn't an authority on what NYSDOL requires,
and stating requirements as fact in a group is how you end up wrong in public
and blamed for it. Describing his own routine is true, useful, and safe.

```text
I got laid off in September. Lack of work — nothing dramatic, the work just ran out.

I'd never filed for unemployment before, so I assumed the application was the hard part. It isn't. The hard part is every week after it.

Here's what my week actually looks like. Certify Sunday. Three work search activities, on three different days, and at least one has to be a priority activity. Log every one of them — company, position, the date — and keep a screenshot. Check the NY.gov inbox daily, because that's where things quietly show up. Keep the phone on during business hours in case they call.

Miss any piece of that and you're the one who has to prove it later.

I was keeping mine in a spreadsheet. It worked until it didn't. I'd apply to something on a Tuesday, forget to write it down, and then be sitting there Sunday morning trying to rebuild my own week out of my browser history. One week I genuinely couldn't remember whether I'd done two or three, and I sat there working out whether to go do another one just to be safe.

So I built a website to keep track of it. You add each application as you make it — where, when, how — and it shows you where you stand for the week. When you need a record, it exports the lot as a PDF or CSV.

It's free, and it stays free. Not a trial, no card, no date where it starts costing you. I'm on unemployment myself, and charging people to prove they looked for work isn't something I'm going to do.

It's new, so there are things wrong with it. That's honestly why I'm posting — I want people to tell me what's broken.

[link]

Happy to answer anything about it.
```

**Open question before posting:** the "three different days, at least one
priority activity" line is the owner's own understanding of his obligation. It
was **not** on `dol.ny.gov/services/ui/wsr` when that page was read on
2026-08-29, which is why the app doesn't carry it as a fact. If it's real it's
likely in the handbook PDF. Worth confirming — but note the post says "here's
what my week looks like", so even if the detail is his own belt-and-braces
habit rather than a rule, the sentence is still true as written.
